Acoustic and clean electric guitar

Ten acoustic and fifteen electric recordings from tonejs-instruments, collected by Nicholaus P. Brosowsky. The source notes identify the University of Iowa Musical Instrument Samples for acoustic guitar and Karoryfer for electric guitar.

Samples are distributed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0. Retrieved from revision 622c2f1c32c8cfce4158ddc3eb26e518ddef37e5. MP3 recordings are unchanged. During playback the app skips leading silence, adjusts level, transposes nearby pitches and applies an envelope. No endorsement is implied. The highest acoustic frets transpose the D5 recording upward and can sound thinner.
